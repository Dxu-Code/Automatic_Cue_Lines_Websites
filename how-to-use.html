How to use Cue

A simple guide. No hard words.

1

Add your script

Open the app. Click "Upload .txt" to add a text file, or click "Edit text" to type your lines by hand.

2

Click a line to learn it

Click on any line in your script. It turns pink. The two lines before it show up as your "cue" — the lines that come right before yours.

3

Use quick select for speed

If your script has names like "MARIA:", you'll see a button for each name. Click a name to grab all of that person's lines at once. You can also type any word in the box and click "Select matches."

4

Use ( ) or [ ] for the important words

Put the words you must memorize inside ( ) or [ ]. Everything else in that line gets a highlight color, so you know it's just extra text, not the part to learn.

5

Pick your highlight color

Use the checkbox and color box in the toolbar to turn the highlight on or off, and to pick any color you like.

6

Practice in Rehearse

Click the "Rehearse" tab. You'll see your cue lines, and your line hidden below. Click "Reveal line" to check yourself. Click "Mark as learned" once you know it.

7

Your work is saved

Everything stays saved in your browser. Close the tab and come back later — your script and your marked lines will still be there.
